Osawa Hayato and Okamoto Yoko promoted at the Kagamibiraki 2019

Guillaume Erard Events 14 January 2019
  • kagamibiraki
  • Osawa Hayato
  • event

The Kagamibiraki ceremony was held on Sunday, January 13, 2019 at the Aikikai Hombu Dojo in Tokyo, and the names of the practitioners promoted to the next grade were made public.

I had the pleasure to be able to share a moment with the newly promoted and this year was special because Osawa Hayato Sensei, one of the teachers whose lessons I most appreciate at the Hombu Dojo, received the 8th dan from the hands of Doshu. Igarashi Kazuo ,another well known instructor to practitioners outside of Japan, was also promoted to the 8th dan.

Osawa Hayato Shihan giving his 8th dan acceptance speech. 

Okamoto Yoko was also promoted to 7th dan this year, a remarkable achievement for one of the few women to achieve this rank. Okamoto Sensei has been a great source of support since I have moved to Japan ten years ago. She was the one who agreed to let me film my first video interview, in spite my lack of experience or credentials, which then allowed me to approach many other Japanese teachers. A number of close students of Miyamoto Sensei and Kumazawa Miyuki all gathered to spend this moment with Okamoto Sensei.

Close friends celebrating a special moment with Okamoto Yoko. 

Note that John Brinsley, who was my sempai in Miyamoto Sensei's class, and John Presley were promoted to 6th and 5th dan, respectively. My friend Michael Thai was also promoted to 5th dan. Doshu gave the traditional hono enbu (奉納演武), which means that the demonstration is a votive offer.

The full list of promotions is available here. Note that John Rogers, Michele Quaranta and Charlie McGinnis received the 7th dan, as well as Malcolm Tiki Shewan, Didier Allouis and Jacques Bonemaison from France. Pascal Guillemin, for his part, received 6th dan.

The traditional nihonshu and mochi to start the year off just right! 

Congratulations to all the new graduates!

Guillaume Erard
