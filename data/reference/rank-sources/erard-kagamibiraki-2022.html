Guillaume Erard Events 10 January 2022

Kagamibiraki 2022 held privately at the Aikikai Hombu Dojo

Guillaume Erard Events 10 January 2022
  • kagamibiraki
  • event

This year again, the Aikido World's major event, which sees the unravelling of the names of the newly promoted persons to the grades of 5th dan and above had to be held in private due to the likely surge of Omicron cases in the greater Tokyo area.

The event indeed usually sees the Aikikai Hombu Dojo at its highest occupation density and the need to hold it in small committee once again made little doubt. Like last year, I was contacted by Hombu in early December as they wanted to know if I would be able to set up a live video stream like last year so that people all around the world could attend the ceremony online.

The layout of the room calls for at least two cameras in order to best frame the event as it unfolds. 

If you missed it, the replay of the event is currently viewable on the Aikikai's YouTube channel and it will remain visible for about a month or so.

I would like to congratulate all newly promoted individuals, notably my fellow Hombu Dojo practitioners Murata Kenzuke (村井 謙介) and Watanabe Yoshikazu (渡邊 吉一) for their promotion to 8th dan, and Murata Shunzo (村田 俊三) for his promotion to 7th dan. I was part of Murata Shunzo san's team for a number of years as part of the organization team for the All Japan Aikido Demonstration and we owe a great deal of excellent pictures to Watanabe san, who excels at photography and consistently puts his skills to use during Aikikai events.

Another notable 8th dan recipient was Robert Frager, who practiced Aikido at Hombu Dojo while O Sensei was still alive. I was also particularly happy to see that Kei Izawa, the vice-chairman of the International Aikido Federation was promoted to 7th dan. Kei is a senior student of the late Kanai Mitsunari (1939–2004) and he has had a tremendous influence on the development of a more global and inclusive vision through his work in the International Aikido Federation over the past decade. Kei was the one who trusted me very early on to help him in his work and it through this that I believe that the Aikikai began to recognize that I could bear such a responsibility of today's stream. Congratulations to Kei and may we collaborate for many more years!
Once again, I was able to count on the help of my friend Mihaly Dobroka, who operated the second camera while I dealt with the stream and the main camera. 

Other friends who were promoted included Philippe Orban, one of Christian Tissier's very close assistants who now teaches in Germany and who was promoted to 7th dan. Sebastien Heurteau and Lijnie Reijers both received the 5th dan, as well as Hombu instructor Satodate Jun. I first met Satodate san when he entered Hombu as a sumikomi shidoin and we spent many hours training together. He has become an excellent practitioner and remained a wonderful individual.

Also of note was the promotion of Peter Brown to 7th dan. Even though I do not know him personally, we crossed path several times while I was training in England within the United Kingdom Aikikai and I visited his Leicester dojo on one occasion back in 2003.

To all those who I might have forgotten, my most sincere congratulations! The full list of promotions is available here.

Guillaume Erard
