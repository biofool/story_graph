## Key Findings for Wikipedia Citation

### Best Independent Sources for Rank Verification

1. **Aikikai suisen PDFs** (already stored) — The gold standard. Official Aikikai Hombu Dojo Kagamibiraki promotion lists. Confirmed: ROBERT NOHA 7th dan (2025 PDF).

2. **Guillaume Erard's Kagamibiraki 2017 article** (`erard-kagamibiraki-2017.html`) — Independent confirmation of Robert Nadeau's 8th dan promotion at Kagamibiraki 2017. Erard is a 6th dan Aikikai instructor who covers Kagamibiraki for the IAF.

3. **USAF Kagamibiraki promotion pages** — Official USAF announcements of Aikikai-recognized promotions for USAF members. Useful for USAF-affiliated subjects.

4. **ASU Kagami Biraki promotion pages** — Official ASU announcements of Aikikai-recognized promotions for ASU members.

5. **Birankai certified teachers list** (`birankai-certified-teachers.pdf`) — Official BNA list of certified instructors with dan ranks.

6. **CAA promotion lists** (already stored) — Official CAA announcements for CAA members. This is the most relevant source for Bob Noha, Robert Nadeau, and Richard Moon, who are all CAA-affiliated.

### Coverage Gaps

- **Bob Noha's 6th dan (~2013)**: No independent online source found confirming the exact date of 6th dan. The Aikido of Petaluma page mentions 5th dan (2000) and 7th dan (2025) but not 6th dan date. The Nadeau Shihan authors page lists him as "6th Dan" (pre-7th dan). The CAA promotion lists (already stored) may contain this.
- **Robert Nadeau's 8th dan**: Confirmed by Erard's 2017 article and Wikipedia. The Aikikai suisen 2017 PDF (if it exists) would be the primary source.
- **Richard Moon's 6th dan**: Only found on publisher/author pages (primary sources). No independent Aikido organization promotion list found. The CAA promotion lists (already stored) may contain this.
- **Peter Ralston**: No Aikido or martial arts dan rank registry records found. He is primarily known as a Cheng Hsin founder and tournament champion, not a dan-ranked practitioner in a traditional system.
- **Dan Millman**: Only Wikipedia mentions shodan in Aikido. No formal registry record found (his training was in the 1970s, before online records existed).
