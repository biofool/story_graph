Aikido seminar scheduled

Hilo Seishikan Aikido will
again present an Aikido Sem-
inar with Shihan Donald S.
Moriyama. Moriyama-sen-
sei is ranked 7-Dan and cur-
rently serves as the Chief
Instructor of the Pearl City
Aikido Dojo.

The seminar is a rare oppor-
tunity to train with one of the
preeminent aikido instruc-
tors in the United States. In the
past year, Moriyama-sensei
conducted aikido seminars
in New Jersey, Nevada and
Hawaii and traveled to Japan
for further training.

Moriyama-sensei began
his aikido training in the ear-
ly 1050s and has trained with
most of the top ranking instruc-
tors, including the late Doshu
Kisshomaru Ueshiba, the late
Morihiro Saito-sensei, and
Doshu Moriteru Ueshiba.

During his tenure in Japan,
he opened a dojo at the Mis-
awa Air Base and also served
as dojo-cho of the Ippai Mori
Dojo at Hachinohe in Aomori-

ken. Moriyama-sensei is also
one of only few certified Iwa-
ma Aikido Bokken and Jo
Instructors outside of Japan and
the only such person desig-
nated in the state of Hawaii.

The class schedule fol-
lows:

—Friday: Youth Class, 6 to
7 p.m.; Open Aikido 1, 7 to9
p.m.
—Saturday: Open Aiki-
do 2, 9 to 11 a.m.; Open Aiki-
do 3, 1 to3 p.m.

Classes will be held at the
Waiakea Recreation Center
and are open to all aikido
practitioners. Hilo Seishikan
Aikido is a not-for-profit orga-
nization offering six different
aikido classes weekly for stu-
dents ranging in age from six
to adult.

For more information about
Moriyama-sensei’s seminar or
Hilo Seishikan Aikido, call
959-8632, email
info @seishikan-aikido.org,
or check out the website at
www.seishikan-aikido.org.

Babe Ruth League sign-up

The Big Island Babe Ruth
League 13-15 division is
currently having open reg-
istration for intermediate
and high school baseball
players for its second half
season.

Players who reside on
the Big Island — including
Puna, Ka‘u, Hamakua and
West Hawaii — are eligible.

League play will start in
April. Players age 16 born
after July 31, 1988 are eli-
gible to participate. High
school junior varsity teams
may submit whole teams.

For registration forms,
call 961-0118. The Big Island
Babe Ruth League has no
restriction on other league
participation.

Cal Ripken League sign-up

The Big Island Cal Rip-
ken League is currently hav-
ing open registration for the
second half season, which
starts in April. All youth
players ages 9-10 & 11-12
and residing on the Big
Island are eligible.

Coach Pitch teams (ages
9 & over) may register
together as a team. Rural

baseball teams and players
are also invited to play.
The Big Island Cal Rip-
ken League is part of the
Babe Ruth League, Inc., the
Hawaii State Cal Ripken
, and the Hawaii State
Babe Ruth League Organi-
zation. To register, call 961-
0118.
