City offers Aikido class

Acity recreation class in beginning Adult Aikido will be held 6:30 to 8
p.m, Tuesday at Ukiah Aikido and Gymnastics. The fee is $35 monthly.
Ages 16 through adult are welcome. Registration forms are available at
City Hall.
