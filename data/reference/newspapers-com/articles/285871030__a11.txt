Class offered
CEDAR CITY — Aikido, the
oriental art of self defense, will be
taught to youth in Cedar City with
classes each Saturday.

Lori Michaelson, manager of
Fiddler's Fitness Center, said
youth ages 8 through 15 are invited
to join and can register at the cen-
ter where the classes will be taught
each Saturday from 12:30 p.m. un-
til 2 p.m.

Instruction for adults will begin
Tuesday, Dec. 2, with one hour
classes each Tuesday and Thurs-
day at 8:30 p.m. Billy Weaver, a
third-degree blackbelt, will be the
teacher.

Michaelson said the monthly
fees for members of the Fiddler’s
Fitness Center will be $10 for youth
and $15 for adults. Non-members’

fees will be $20 for youth and $25
for adults.
