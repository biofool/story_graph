CITY HALL NEWS

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS

Stricter rules may be on way
for garbage can placement

Legislation passed in July that
fined residents for having their gar-
bage receptacles on the sidewalk
was amended Thursday during the
City Operations and Neighborhood
Services Committee meeting.

Residents would now be required
to keep their receptacles out of pub-
lic view or face a fine. Fines range
depending on the offense but would
start at $80.

The Department of Public Works
said they would work with residents
on a case-by-case basis to determine
extenuating circumstances.
