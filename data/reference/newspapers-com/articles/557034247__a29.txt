Aikido Seminar slated in Hilo

Hilo Seishikan Aikido
will once again present an
Aikido Seminar with Shi-
han Donald S. Moriyama.
Moriyama-sensei is
ranked 7-Dan and current-
ly serves as the Chief
Instructor of the Pearl City
Aikido Dojo.

This seminar is a rare
opportunity to train with
one of the preeminent
aikido instructors in the
United States. In the past
year, Moriyama-sensei
conducted aikido seminars
in New Jersey, Nevada and
Hawaii and traveled to
Japan for further training.

Moriyama-sensei began
his aikido training in the
- early 1950s and has
trained with most of the
top ranking instructors
including late Doshu
Kisshomaru Ueshiba, the
late Morihiro Saito-sensei
and Doshu Moriteru
Ueshiba. During his tenure
in Japan, he opened a dojo
at the Misawa Air Base
and also served as dojo-
cho of the Ippai Mori Dojo
at Hachinobe in Aomori-
ken.

Moriyama-sensei _is
also one of only few certi-
fied Iwama Aikido
Bokken and Jo Instructors
outside of Japan and the
only such person designat-
ed in the state of Hawaii.

The schedule of classes
is as follows:

Friday, July 23

—yYouth Class: 6-7
p.m., Open Aikido 1: 7-9
p.m.

Saturday, July 24

—Open Aikido 2: 8-11
a.m.; Open Aikido 3:
12:30-3 p.m.

Classes will be held at
Waiakea Recreation Cen-
ter and are open to all aiki-
do practitioners. Hilo
Seishikan Aikido is a not-
for-profit organization
offering six different aiki-
do classes weekly for stu-
dents ranging in age from
six to adult.

For more information
about Moriyama-sensei’s
seminar or Hilo Seishikan
Aikido, call 959-8632,
email info @seishikan-
aikido.org or check out
our website at
www.seishikan-
aikido.org.
