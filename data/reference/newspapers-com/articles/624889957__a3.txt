Women’s self-defense

An introductory workshop in self-de-
fense for women will be held from 1 to 5
p-m. at River City Aikido, 2358
Fruitridge Road. The specialized course
will teach women basic techniques on
how to protect. themselves from an
unarmed assailant. Cost for the work-
shop is $15. For more information, call
362-5753.
