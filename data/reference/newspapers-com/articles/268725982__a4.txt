SANDERS BEACH

Martial arts

focus of seminar

Hiroshi Ikeda Sensei is
scheduled to present a
martial arts seminar Oct.

South I St.

Hiroshi Ikeda is the
founder and chief instruc-
tor of Boulder Aikikai in
Boulder, Colo. He has been
a student of the aikido
school of martial arts for
32 years.

The cost for the seminar
is $70 or $25 per session.
