RACES — 12th annual Great Ski Race, the
West's largest cross-country race, is scheduled for
Sunday, 30K from Tahoe City to Truckee, $18,
(916) 583-0484. Pre-registration is required for the
Big Valley Ski Cup series race at Kirkwood on
Sunday, (next races are March 20 at Northstar-at-
Tahoe and March 27 at Heavenly), register at most
area ski shops and clubs. The U.S. National Cross
Country Championships will be held at Royal
Gorge March 13-20 and will include senior, mas-
ters and disabled divisions, (916) 426-3871. The
9th California Gold Rush 50K and Silver Rush 25K
cross country races will be held March 20 at Royal
Gorge, $25-$35, 9 a.m., (916) 426-3871. Corpo-
rate recreational series racing will be held March
25-27 at Kirkwood, (415) 243-0128. Yosemite Nor-
dic Holiday Race on Saturday at Badger Pass,
$10, (209) 372-1244; Tahoe Donner is sponsoring
a 5K night race, $4 (or $9 with pasta dinner) on
March 9, 7 p.m., (916) 587-9487. The Snowshoe

Mail Run 12K race is scheduled for
March 12, 10 a.m. at Hope Valley, $12, (916) 694-
2475. A Tripie Ski Challenge will be held March 12
at Tahoe Donner, $5, followed by the Karhu Cali-
fornia 25K race on March 13, $12, 10 a.m. both
days, (916) 587-9484. The 15th annual Echo to
Kirkwood race will be held March 26, 10 a.m., 14
miles, $40 per team, (209) 258-6000. “Tom's 10K
Classic” will be held April 3 at Bear Valley, $10 by
March 31, (209) 753-2834.

CLINICS — High performance, recreational and
telemark clinics for all levels of ability are available
at Northstar-at-Tahoe, (916) 587-0271. Royal
Gorge offers a cross country “Race Camp” on
April 21-24, $270 by April 1, includes lodging,
meals and instruction, (916) 426-3871.
