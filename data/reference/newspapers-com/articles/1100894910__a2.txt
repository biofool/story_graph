PUBLIC NOTICE
State Department of Health Services

Legal Notice of Intent to Issue
A Hazardous Waste Facility Permit

The Department of Health Services, Toxic
Substances Control Division, North Coast
California Section has drafted a Hazardous
Waste Facility Permit for the following facility:
Marin County Department of Agriculture
5600 Nicasio Valley Road
Nicasio, California 94946

The permit will authorize this facility to store
hazardous wastes under specific conditions to
assure safe operation of the facility.

This notice hereby invites comments before
final issuance of this permit. Anyone wishing to
comment on this permit should do so by submit-
ting written comments within’ the next 45 days
to the address listed below. All comments will be
taken into consideration in preparing the final
permit. All comments will be addressed in
writing in the Administrative Record and will be
available to the general public.

A public hearing on this facility may be re-
quested. Any such request shall be written and
submitted to the address below. This request
shall clearly delineate all concerns and/or inade-
quacies in the permit and shall state the specific
reasons for conducting a public hearing.

The draft permit, application forms, operation
plan and supplements submitted as required are
available for public review at North Coast
California Section Office, Toxic Substances
Control Division, Department of Health Ser-
vices, 2151 Berkeley Way, Room 119, Berkeley,
CA. 94704 between the hours of 8:00 a.m. and
5:00 p.m., Monday through Friday, telephone
(415)540-2043. Written comments should also be
sent to this address. Contact person: Wil Bruhns,
Permit Coordinator.
