‘Happy Martial Arts’
fete set at Stanford

A “Festival of the Happy Martial Arts” will be held
Sunday afternoon, May 21, at Stanford University’s
Frost Amphitheater.

The festival will be held from 1 to 5:30 p.m. Thereis
an admission charge,

The events include demonstrations by martial arts
masters, involving multiple attacks and sword
dances. The co-sponsors are the International
Movement Arts Center and the Stanford Aikido and
Karate Clubs,

The moderator will be George Leonard, an aikido
black belt and author, and the festival features Mit-
sugi Saotome, master instructor of aikido, Robert
Nadeau, Al Chung-liang Huang, Wendy Palmer and
Mark Mir.
