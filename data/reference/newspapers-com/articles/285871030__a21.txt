ST. GEORGE — Ron Hunt of Ce-
dar City won the 1986 St. George
Open Horseshoe Tournament last
weekend. He finished with a 7-0 re-
cord and a 53.4 ringer percentage in
Class A competition.

Other class play winners at the
tournament were Wendel Bundy of
Cedar City in Class B, Lew Brown of
St.*George — winner of a five-way
plaVoff — in Class C, Les Cole of Hur-
ricane in Class D, and Dan Haycock
of Fredonia in Class E. Reigning Ju-
mors Girls State Champion Penny
Bundy of St. George won the Junior
Class Championship.

Phe St. George tournaient was
one of three held in the region during
November. Willard Napier of Seal
Beach, Calif., was the winner of the
Peppermill Resort Horsehoe Holiday
Tournament and Diane Lopez of Los
Angeles, Calif., won the Hacienda

La

Hotel-Bud Lite Tournament in Las
Vegas.

Many players from the southern
Utah area participated in all three
tournaments.

In other results from the St.
George tourney, Eileen Serna of New
Mexico was second and Dennis Ohms
of Cedar City was third in Class A.
Following Bundy in Class B were
George Sarkis of Cedar City and Ja-
son Bundy of Fredonia.

Verle Leavitt of Gunlock took sec-
ond in Class C with Joe Lester of New
Mexico grabbing third. In Class B,
Gene Brown of Hurricane was sec-
ond and Reed Lowe of St. George was
third. Second in Class E was Glen
McAllister and third went to Doug
Alger of Mesquite. Runner-up in the
Junior Class was Belinda Bundy of
St. George with Doug Haycock of
Fredonia third.
