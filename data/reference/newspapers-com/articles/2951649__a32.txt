Robert L. Winslow

A memorial service was held for Robert L. Winslow. Winslow died
Jan. 31, 1996 after a long illness, at the age of 69.

Winslow was a former Mendocino County assistant district attorney,
and Justice and Superior Court judge. Judge Winslow retired as a senior
partner in the Century City law firm of Snella and Manella.

He is survived by his wife, Betty Winslow; sons, David Winslow and
Spencer Winslow; daughters, Leslie Neger and Allison Duff; and three
grandchildren.
