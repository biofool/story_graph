CEDAR CITY — Aikido, the orien-
tal art of self defense, will be taught
to youth in Cedar City with classes
beginning today.

Lori Michaelson, manager of
Fiddler’s Fitness Center, said youth
ages 8 through 15 are invited to join
and can register at the center where
the classes will be taught each Satur-
day from 12:30 p.m. until 2 p.m.

Instruction for adults will begin
Tuesday, Dec. 2, with one hour
classes each Tuesday and Thursday
at 8:30 p.m. Billy Weaver, a third-de-
gree blackbelt, will be the teacher.

Michaelson said the monthly fees
for members of the Fiddler’s Fitness

- Center will be $10 for youth and $15
for adults. Non-members’ fees will
be $20 for youth and $25 for adults.
