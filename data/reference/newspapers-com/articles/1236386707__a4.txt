Drug-treatment
center neighbors
are still unhappy

Neighbors of a north Sacramento drug-
treatment house will try again tonight to

| persuade the City Council to move the center

from their neighborhood.

The residents who live near the Aquarian
Effort facility at 1550 Juliesse Ave. will
appeal a decision last month by the city
Planning Commission granting the center a
year extension of a special permit to operate.

The commission, after listening to testimo-
ny from neighbors — complaining the cen-
ter’s clients wander their neighborhood, often
searing residents — and from police — who
said the number or type of calls they have
received did not worsen — decided the center
created no great hardship in the area.

Residents nearby said the center’s officials
do not supervise the clients enough and
cannot keep them from roaming the neighbor-
hood.

But directors of the 38-person detoxification
and drug rehabilitation house told city offi-
cials they have tightened security and super-
vision and have installed fences, locks and
alarms.

The permit granted by the planning com-
mission requires that Aquarian Effort offi-
cials meet with neighbors every three
months, develop a recreation plan for clients
and return to renew their permit in a year.
