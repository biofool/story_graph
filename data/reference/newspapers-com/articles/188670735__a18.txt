Critic’s choice /Popular music

Bob Dylan is bringing it back home again. It’s been nine long years
since he performed in his native state, but-he's coming back in a
big way. Thursday he will share the stage with the Grateful Dead at
the first-ever concert in the Metrodome. Dylan will be backed by
Tom Petty and the Heartbreakers, who also will perform some of
their hits without Dylan. The Dead have not played in the Twin
Cities in four or five years. The enduring San Francisco group opens
at 6.p.m. with Dylan and the Heartbreakers scheduled to hit the
stage after 9:30 p.m. Expect the Dead to carry on for three hours
and Dylan and the Heartbreakers to play 21 hours. Bring your
binoculars. Several thousand tickets, priced at $20, are still
aveilable at Wax Museum and Great American Music stores. For
tickets, call’333-5577.

— Jon Bream
