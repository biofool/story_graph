BOARD OF SUPERVISORS OF THE
COUNTY OF MARIN
Ordinance No. 2867

An Ordinance of the County of Marin, State of
California Amending Title 22 (Zoning) of the
Marin County Code by Reclassifying AP.
#170-130-10 and 170-162-06

The Board of Supervisors of the County of
Marin does hereby ordain as follows:
Section I: Title 22 (Zoning) of the Marin County
Code is hereby amended by reclassifying
Assessor’s Parcels 170-130-10 and 170-162-06
from R-1:B-4 (Single Family Residential, One
Unit Per Acre) and RSP-0.05 (Residential, Single
Family Planned, One Unit Per 20 Acres) to
RSP-0.09 (Residential, Single Family Planned:
One Unit per 11 Acres). -
Section II: This ordinance shall be and is hereby
declared to be in full force and effect as of thirty
(30) days from and after the date of its passage
and shall be published once before the expiration
of fifteen (15) days after its passage, with the
names of the Supervisors voting for and against
the same in The Point Reyes Light, a newspaper
of general circulation published in the County of
Marin.
Passed and adopted at a regular meeting at the
Board of Supervisors of the County of Marin,
State of California, on the 12th day of February,
1985 by the following vote to-wit:
Ayes: Supervisors: Al Aramburu, Gary
Giacomini, Harold Brown, Jr., Bob Stockwell
Noes: Supervisors: —
Absent: Supervisors: Bob Roumiguiere
Abstain: Supervisors: —

Robert B. Stockwell

Chairman of the Board of Supervisors
