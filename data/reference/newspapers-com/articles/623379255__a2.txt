Dillon Beach: Many limits of

New Hogan: Bass are still the
best bet here, with the fishing action
only fair. Bass are in the top 10 feet
of water, from the mouth of the river
up through the island campgrounds.
Fishermen are taking spinner baits,
minnows and jigs averaging 2 to 3
pounds. Stripers are 25 to 30 feet
down, averaging 8 pounds and hit-
ting drifted minnows. A few catfish
are being taken at the mouth of the
river on anchovies and liver. (209)
772-1462.

New Melones: Trout fishing
has slowed a little. A two- to three-
fish-per-rod average has dropped to
one to two fish. Bank fishermen are
still topping the boaters for produc-
tion. Rainbows are taking night-
crawlers floated off the bottom with
air or a marshmallow. Top spots
have been Angel's Cove and Glory
Hole Cove. Catfish are being taken
beneath the Highway 49 bridge.
Panfish are starting to show, from
20 feet down to the surface. (209)
728-2166.
