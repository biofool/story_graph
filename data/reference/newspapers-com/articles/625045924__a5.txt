Arriving just before Election Day

Specifically, they have landed on
the doorstep of developer Gregg Luk-

any involvement in the campaign.
However, The Bee has learned that
Lukenbill’s spokesman approached a
printer just three weeks before the
election about producing a campaign
brochure.

That printer declined, but an anti-
ballpark mailer was produced, and
paid for by a steel manufacturef «in
