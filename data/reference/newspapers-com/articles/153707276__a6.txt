The Great Western Melodrama:
The Tumbleweed Gang and Sparks Little the-
ater will present the free interactive show at
8:30 p.m. Oct. 19 at the Great Basin Brewing
Co., 846 Victorian Ave. in Sparks. Details:
355-7711. Additional performances will be
Oct. 21 at the Sparks Library Meeting Room,
Oct. 26 at the Children’s Museum of Northern
Nevada and Oct. 30 and 31 at Ramada
Vacation Suites. Details: 356-8331.

The Magic Flute: Evelyn De La Rosa and
David Small star in the Nevada Opera produc-
tion of “The Magic Flute” Oct. 23 and 25 at
the Pioneer Center for the Performing Arts,
100 S. Virginia St. Showtime is 7:30 p.m, Oct.
23 and 8 p.m. Oct. 25. The opera will be per-
formed in English, Prior to the Oct, 23 show, a
PrimaDiners dinner catered by Napa-Sonoma
will be served in the Pioneer Exhibit Hall. Cost
for the dinner is $25. For details, call the
Nevada Opera, 786-4046.

The Haunted Pirate: Bruka Theater for
Children will present the play at 10 a.m. Oct.
23, 24 and 25 and at 2 p.m. Oct. 25. Cost: $7
adults. $5 seniors and children. The cost for
groups of 10 or more is $4 per person. Details:
323-3221.

Dracula: Reno Little Theater will present
the play adapted from the Bram Stoker novel
at 8 p.m. Fridays and Saturdays and at 2 p.m.
Sundays Oct. 24-Novy. 2 at the Elks Lodge, 597
Kumle Lane. On Oct. 31. there will be a mid-
night show. Cost: $12, $10 students and
seniors. Details: 329-0661 or 331-1877.

Grimm's Fairy Tales: Renaissance

at the Virginia City High School. Additional
shows will be held at 8 p.m. Oct. 25 and at 2
p.m. Oct 26 at the Sierra Arts Foundation.
Details: 841-5960.
