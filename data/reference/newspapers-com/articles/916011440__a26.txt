LOCAL BOWLING NOTEBOOK

Shafer bowls over competition

By Jim Rathgeber
The Idaho Statesman

Boise’s Robert Shafer had a
big performance in league and
tournament competition last
week.

The smooth-swinging  right-
hander topped the 1,000 mark in
the four-game Astro Classic
league last Monday at Westy’s
Garden Lanes, had a 692 three-
game set in the Pioneer League
on Wednesday at Westy’s, and
finished fifth in the Grand Prix
Bowling Association Pepsi
Showcase Finals over the week-
end at Bowl-ero Lanes in Idaho
Falls.

In the Astro Classic, Shafer
ended with a 1,014 set, which
included a 290 game, while he
had a 248 high en route to the
692 in the-Pioneer League.

Shafer opened the stepladder
finals in the Grand Prix. Finals
with a 214-205 victory over Russ
Hunt of Ogden, Utah, but then
lost 248-244 to Mills. In the six-
game qualifying round, Shafer
was fifth at 1,480 including bo-
nus pins.

Dave Symes of Salt Lake City
claimed the Grand Prix title by
defeating top-seeded Troy Ware-
ing of Salt Lake City 248-206 in
the championship match. In ear-
lier matches, Symes beat Wes
Mills of Idaho Falls 215-197 and
edged Idaho Falls’ Paul Kreins

266-258.

Symes pocketed $950, Wareing
won $500, Kreins earned $400,
Mills received $300, Shafer won
$270 and Hunt got $250.

Other area bowlers finishing
in the top 24: Robb Checketts,
Kuna, ninth, $180; Dave Dietz,
Nampa, 10th, $175; Bill Hewlett,
Boise, 12th, $165; Gene Lampe
Jr., Boise, 13th, $155; and Kevin
Grupa, Mountain Home Air
Force Base, 16th, $140..

It took a score of 1,249 to
make the cut to the top 24. War-
eing led the way with a 1,677
total including bonus pins.

Wareing was the hottest play-
er en route to earning his No. 1
seeding as he rolled two 300
games. Craig Goodwin of Idaho
Falls had a 290, and there were
also games of 289, 279 (2), 278,
277 and 270.

The GPBA Bowler of the Year
Award was presented to Kreins
for being the tour’s best all-
around competitor for the sea-
son, while the second Darin L.
Youngman Award — for the
highest average — went to War-
weng. He averaged 214.16 for
the year on the tour.

@ HIGH MEN’S SCORES: The
second high men’s four-game
block during the week was a 970
by Grupa in the Pro-Am Classic
league at Meridian Lanes. He
had 724 for his first three games.

Tom Hubbard had the high for

the week in three-game league
action, hitting 749 with the help
of a 278 game in the Bench Con-
struction League on Thursday
at Westy’s.

Mark Phelps, Dee Loveland
and John Hanna all topped the
700 mark in the Men’s Twilight
League on Wednesday at Merid-
ian Lanes. Phelps finished with
a 733 set, Loveland had 730 and
Hanna had 723.

Other 700-plus series included:
718 by Norm Johnston in the
Mall League at Twentieth Cen-
tury Lanes; 717 by Ron Arndt in
his first three games in Pro-Am
play; 710 by John Murphy in the
Big 12 League at Westy’s, and
705 by Craig Frazee in the
Wednesday Scramblers League
at Westy’s.

@ HIGH WOMEN’S SCORES:
The high three- and four-game
series of the week in women’s
play were by left-hander Johnna
Dunten. She had 690 for the first
three games and 904 for four in
Pro-Am action at Meridian
Lanes.

The week’s high game was a
261 by Sioux Wieckowicz, on the
way to a 682 three-game block in
the Weekenders League on Fri-
day at Westy’s. .

Jackie Thomas had a pair of
800-plus four-game sets — 861 in
the Pro-Am league at Meridian
and 832 in the All Stars League
at Westy’s.
