Exhibits Saturday and Sunday include

April 22 is the day
to thank secretaries

Bosses will honor their secretaries at a
celebration April 22, from 4 to 7 p.m. at the
Carousel, 2200 Stephens Ave.

The event is sponsored by the University
of Montana, the city of Missoula, Missoula
County and the Chamber of Commerce.

Business people planning to invite their
secretaries should purchase tickets by
Thursday. Tickets are $5, which includes
beer and hors d'oeuvres. They are on sale at
the chamber, mayor's office, the county
commissioners’ office and at the UM Associ-
ated Students’ Store and the UM Lodge.

Martial art seminar,
demonstration offered

An aikido seminar is under way at the
University of Montana and will include a
public demonstration Wednesday.

Aikido, also called the dancing martial
art, emphasizes self-defense and using an
opponent’s energy against him. It was estab-
lished in Japan about 70 years ago and is re-
lated to judo and karate.

The visiting instructor is Hiroshi Ikeda, a
fifth-degree black belt.

The seminar is held at the UM Old
Men’s Gym through Thursday from 7 to 9
p.m. each day. The public demonstration
will start at 7 p.m. Wednesday.

The seminar is open to people with no
previous experience, according to local ai-
kido instructors. Cost is $6 per session or $20
for all sessions. For more information call
Mark Bakeman at 721-4439.
