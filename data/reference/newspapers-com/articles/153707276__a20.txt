American Heart Association is offering a
1998 Summer Student Research Program
in which students will be assigned to a
cardiovascular research laboratories in
California, Nevada or Utah for 10 weeks
to work under experienced scientists.
Students will receive a $2,500 stipend.
Students must be enrolled full-time in a
college or university: attend an institution
in California, Nevada or Utah, or be a
resident of one of those states: have
junior or senior status by fall 1998; have
completed at least four semesters or six
quarters of biological sciences, physics or
chemistry, and have completed at least
one quarter of calculus, statistics, compu-
tational methods or computer science.
Applications must be requested by Dec.
15, and are available at the local Heart
Association office, 1181 Terminal Way,
#111. Applications must be received by
Jan. 15. Details: 322-7064.
