TRAINER PROFILE:

Ben Davis joined
Focus Fitness after a
long search for Stew-
art's perfect protégé.
Stewart hopes to add
one more trainer,
preferably a female
clone of Ben.

Ben is an allAmerican good guy with a
genuine interest in helping his clients
make positive changes in their fitness
levels. He can recite the scientific name
of each muscle group and tell you
exactly how and why your body digests
food the way it does. For instance, did
you realize it takes the human body
almost 72 hours to digest a juicy steak?
Most regular meals only require a few
hours, but red meat requires a few
days. It’s definitely not pillow talk, but
it’s useful information that | never real-
ized | wanted to know.
