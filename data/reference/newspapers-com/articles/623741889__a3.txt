FREE RUNNING CLINICS — The Sacra-
mento Long Distance Running Association
offers a free running clinic every Thursday
until the California International Marathon
on Dec. 6. The clinic begins at 6 p.m. at
American River Hospital. For more informa-
tion, call 447-2786.

SUTTER BUTTES WALK — A variety of
walk/hike programs are available through
February. Call Ira Heinrich in Chico (916)
343-6614.

4TH ANNUAL FUN-RUN/WALK — Satur-
day, 8 a.m. at Oak Park United Methodist
Church, 3600 Broadway, $9 donation, 456-
0487.

BRIDGE TO BRIDGE RUN — Sunday, 9
a.m. at the Ferry Building, San Francisco; 8
miles and 5k; (415) 951-7070.

PACIFIC SWIM AND SPORT BIATHLON
— Sunday, 9 a.m. at Beale's Point, Folsom;
1%-mile swim, 6.2-mile run; $25 iron and
$35 relay; 442-7946 days, 924-8311 eve-
nings.

DAM TOUGH RUN — Sunday at Lake Isa-
bella auxiliary dam (35-miles northeast of
Bakersfield); relay and ultra-marathon,
38.6-miles; (619) 379-5236.

FALL MALL FUN RUN — Sunday, 8 a.m.,
County Fair Mall, Woodland, 5k and 10-mile
run/walk, $5, contact the Woodland Recre-
ation Department.

SIERRA NEVADA 50-MILE RUN — Oct. 3,
7 a.m. at Granite Bay, $50, 965-8326.

WILDCAT TRIATHLON — Oct. 3 at Tilden
Park, Berkeley, 5m swim, 5k run and 15m
bike, 966-6185.

11TH ANNUAL SACRAMENTO MARA-
THON — Oct. 4, 7 a.m. at William Land
Park; TAC-certified 26-mile, 385-yard
course, $20. Call 488-7181 (days), 678-
5005 (evenings and weekends) and (707)
448-9635 Vacaville/Fairfield area.

3RD ANNUAL CALIFORNIA MILE — Oct. 4
at Mark Hopkins Hotel, Nob Hill, San Fran-
cisco, (415) 383-0314.

RUSH FOR THE GOLD — Oct. 10-11, 8
a.m. at River Park, Coloma; (Oct. 10)
5k/10k run and Y2-mile children’s run, 3.5-
mile kayak sprint; (Oct. 11) 60-mile bike
race; $10 each event, 622-6919.

FIRE ENGINE TO FIRE ENGINE FUN RUN
— Oct. 10, Old Sacramento, 4th annual 6k
run, 449-5708.

4TH ANNUAL BASS DERBY RUN — Oct.
10, 8 a.m., City Park (8rd and Montezuma
streets) Rio Vista, $7 until Oct. 5, 5k/10k,
(707) 374-2700.

8TH ANNUAL ZOO ZOOM — Oct. 11, 8:30
a.m. at the Sacramento Zoo, 5/10k
run/walk, $6 for under 12 and $9.50 for
adults; day of race $7.50 for under 12 and
$12 for adults; 449-5166.
