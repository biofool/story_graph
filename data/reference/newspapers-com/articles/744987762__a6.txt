What is Shintaikido Massage?
Based on Asian Massage Tech-
niques more than 3,000 years old,
Shintaikido Massage Therapy com-
bines traditional techniques along
with deep tissue and acupressure
(shiatsu). Some people think of mas-
sage as a luxury, and turn to it only
vat times of dire need, But given
modern pressures and the increase
in stress-related illnesses, Dave be-
lieves that Shintaikido Massage
Therapy should be an integral part
of everyday life. Shintaikido Mas-
sage Therapy can help with strained

muscles and joints, aching back and
shoulders from too much sitting,
work and sports injuries, over-
strained muscles from physical la-
bor orexcessive exercise, headaches,
fatigue, circulatory problems from
lack of exercise, along with effec-
live acupressure treatment of cer-
tain respiratory, neuromuscular, and
digestive disorders, Unlike a tradi-
tional “rubdown,” Shintaikido Mas-
sage Therapy works with not only

the physical, but the energy status of

the client, This allows the session to
reach a deeper level of healing.
There are three components to
Shintaikido Massage Therapy. The
most comforting treatment is the
Energy Balancing Massage. Relax
for a half or full session while your
stress melts away. The Energy Bal-
ancing Massage is usually done with
the aid of oil or lotion, It incorpo-
rates stroking and kneading of the
muscles, and warming of the skin.
The second is Okazaki Long Life
Massage Therapy, using proven
techniques more than 1,000 years
old, which continue to be very effec-
tive in treating muscle and joint ail-
ments along with chronic nerve prob-
lems. Okazaki Long Life Massage
Therapy is a deep tissue type of
massage that incorporates the major
acupoints points involved in healing
the body and balancing the energy.
Shintaikido Acupressure is rooted
in the original Chinese form of

shiatsu done with the thumbs, fin-
gers, forearms, and elbows applying
pressure to specific acupoints.
Shintaikido Acupressure can be done
without oil. It can be performed di-
rectly on the skin, or through the
clothing. In the treatment of ail-
ments, moxibustion and cupping
may be applied to the acupoints.

The Energy Balancing Massage,
Okazaki Long Life Massage
Therapy, and  Shintaikido
Acupressure can be rigorous and
invigorating or more soothing, with-
out losing their therapeutic value.
Whether your goal is pain relief,
increased flexibility or simple re-
laxation, Shintaikido Massage
Therapy has something for every-
one. You may request a specific
massage or a combination of differ-
ent Shintaikido Massage Therapy
styles.
