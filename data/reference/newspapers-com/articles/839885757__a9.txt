Areher-Smith nuptials
celebrated in Palo Alto

The newly wed Mr. and Mrs.
Robert Eugene Smith, Cubber-
ley High School and Foothill
College graduates, have estab-
lished their first home in Red-
wood City after a honeymoon
in Las Vegas and Disneyland.

The bride is the former Dei-
trah Lea Archer, daughter of
the Roy E. Archers of Palo
Alto.

She exchanged nuptial vows
with Robert recently in the
Covenant Presbyterian Church
in Palo Alto.

The bridegroom, son of Mr.
and Mrs. Donald C. Smith of
Santa Clara, expects to re-

ceive his degree in business
administration from the Uni-
versity of California at Berke-
ley in December,

His bride is employed in the
engineering department of Pa-
cific Telephone.

The bride’s maid of honor
was Rita Archer, her sister.
Bridesmaids were Sharon
Smith of Santa Clara and Lin-
da Mood of Cupertino, Flower
girl was Cindi Cochran.

The bridezroom’s best man
was his father. Ushers were
Pete Landsbergen of Atherton
and Gordon Manchester Jr. of
San Carlos.
