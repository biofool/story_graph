Business Briefs

Aikido
classes in
Pacifica

Sensei Dave Bendigkeit,
chief instructor and owner
of Shintaikido of Pacifica,
is now offering classes in
Aikido. “Aikido” means the
“Way of Harmony with the
Spirit.” It is a martial art
that teaches one to blend
with and use the force of
the attacker to control him
without contributing to the
creation of violence.

Aikido is a Japanese mar-
tial art, which was founded
in the 1940s by O-Sensei
Morihei Ueshiba. O-Sensei
was a visionary who cre-
ated an art which embodied
a philosophy of protection
for all beings. This included
even those who would do us
harm. True Japanese Budo,
Aikido training helps the
student to understand the
roots of the martial spirit and
to perform the most difficult
techniques in the martial
arts — those involving get-
ting out of harm’s way and
protecting your attacker.
Aikido is a philosophy with
physical expression. The
unification of mind, body,
and spirit creates a clarity
and calmness which trans-
fers to daily life.

In answer to many re-
quests over the years, Sen-
sei Dave Bendigkeit is very

happy to be able to offer
these classes in Aikido. He
began his training in Aikido
in 1967 with Robert Nadeau
Sensei at the Mountain
View Aiki Kai, and trained
throughout the succeed-
ing years, finally becoming
a student of Patricia Hen-
dricks Sensei at Aikido of
San Leandro and receiving
his Black Belt. Hendricks
Sensei helped greatly in giv-
ing Sensei Dave a firm grasp
of the concept of basic to
advanced flow of technique.
He will be passing on this
approach to his students.
Sensei Dave Bendigkeit
says: “I would like to ex-
press my sincere gratitude
to my Aikido teacher, Patri-
cia Hendricks Shihan, who
was kind enough to tell me
that she feels that my teach-

ing comes ‘from a place of
integrity.’ It is the highest
honor for me to receive her
blessing for my teaching
this art.”

Classes for adults are
Tuesdays and Thursdays
from 7:10 to 8 p.m. Adults
are welcome to stay for the
Sword class which follows
the Aikido class. Classes
for youth (ages 8 & up) are
Mondays and Wednesdays
from 3:15 to 4 p.m. In ad-
dition to these new Aikido
classes, Sensei Dave offers
regular classes in the mar-
tial art of Shintaikido &
Kajukenbo for adults, Ninja
Turtles (4 & 5 year olds),
Shintaikido Rangers (6 & 7
year olds). Classes are held
at Shintaikido of Pacifica,
150 Eureka Square Shop-
ping Center.
