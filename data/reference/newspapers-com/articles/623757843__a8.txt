SACRAMENTO BIKE HIKERS — Saturday, 9 a.m.,
meet the Guy West Bridge for an easy 25-mile new-
comers ride, Mike 334-8561; Sunday, 9 a.m., meet at
the K Mart parking lot on the corner of Watt Avenue
and Elkhorn Boulevard in North Highlands. It's an easy
38-mile ride covering parts of Antelope. Roseville
Rocklin and Stanford. Bring water and money for
snacks. Harry, 332-3332

SACRAMENTO WHEELMEN — Sierra Century XIV

and the 31-C/B ride begins at 8 a.m., Jim 624-8155;
Bring water and snacks on Sunday's 30-mile ride be-
ginning at 10 a.m. at the Granite Creek Shopping Cen-

BART station (west parking lot) at 10 a.m. on April 24
for a 55-mile ride around Mt. Diablo. Lots of small
rolling hills and one long climb. Jeff (415) 654-4025
