SACRAMENTO WHEELMEN — Cameron
Park Evening Ride tonight, Cameron Park
exit off Hwy 50, 5:30 p.m., meet across
from Burger King, 726-8486. “Spooky
Ride,” Saturday, 10 a.m., 20 miles, meet at
3435 J Street for this easy but haunting
ride, 444-8051. Rocklin Ride on Sunday, 10

488-8218.

LAKE MEADOWS MOUNTAIN RIDE —
Nov. 14, three hours of mostly flat terrain
for views of Mt. Tallac, Freel Peak and An-

State Park system. Meet at the Lake Tahoe
Country Club parking lot in Meyers near
Hwy 50. Bring a camera and mountain bike.
(916) 525-7277.
