ORANGE RYE BREAD

3 tablespoons oil

3 tablespoons honey

14% cups milk

1 teaspoon anise seeds

1 tablespoon grated orange
rind (preferably undyed)

114 cups whole wheat flour
1 cup rye flour

1 teaspoon salt

1 tablespoon baking powder
4 cup wheat germ

1 egg, separated

Preheat oven to 350 degrees. Grease an 8x4-inch loaf pan. Sift the
flours, salt and baking powder together, then add the wheat germ. Beat
the egg yolk into the oil and honey. Add the milk, seeds and orange rind.
Beat the egg white until stiff. Sift the dry ingredients into the liquids just
enough to mix. Fold in the egg white. Spread mixture in the loaf pan and
bake about 45 minutes.

RAISIN RYE BREAD

1 cup raisins

1 cup water

2 teaspoons active dry yeast
Y% cup warm water

3 cups whole rye flour

4 cups whole wheat flour

\% teaspoon caraway seeds

3 tablespoons molasses

2 tablespoons cider vinegar

2 tablespoons oil

Raisin water plus additional
water if needed

1 teaspoon salt

Cook the raisins for 5 minutes in 1 cup water. Drain, reserving the
liquid to use as part of the water measure. Dissolve the yeast in the 4
cup warm water. Stir the dry ingredients into a mixing bowl. Combine
the molasses, vinegar, oil and raisin water in a 2-cup measure and add
cold water to total 2 cups. Mix the yeast and the other liquids into the |,
flour to make a somewhat stiff dough. Knead well, using water on the
table and on your hands to soften the dough. After about 10 minutes,
before the dough becomes sticky, flatten it out on the board, spread the
raisins on it, fold it up and knead the raisins in. Stop kneading when the
dough becomes a little sticky. Form the dough into a ball and place it
smooth side up in the bowl. Cover and keep at warm room temperature
in a draft-free place. After about an hour and a half, gently poke the
center of the dough 14-inch deep with your wet finger. If the hole doesn’t
fill in at all or the dough sighs, it’s ready for the next step. Press flat,
form into a smooth round, and let the dough rise once more as before.
The second rising will take about half as long as the first. Press the
dough flat and divide in two. Round it and let it rest until relaxed, then
deflate and shape into loaves. Let them rise in a warm, draft-free place
until the dough slowly returns a gently made fingerprint. Bake about an
hour at 350 degrees. Makes 2 loaves.
