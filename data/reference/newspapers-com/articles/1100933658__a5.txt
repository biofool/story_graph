Autumn classes at the Dance Palace in
Point Reyes Station begin next week with a
schedule that is more extensive and more
varied than ever before.

You can get full details on fees and regis-
tration from the new Dance Palace Bulletin
or by calling 663-1075. You can also see
various classes demonstrated and hear them
described by the instructors in a free ”"Show-
case” program at the Dance Palace that
begins at 7:30 p.m. Saturday.

Here, in brief, is the schedule:

MONDAYS

Creative Dance for ages five through
eight, taught by Carol Friedman, 3:15 to
4 p.m. (Oct. 17 to Nov. 15S).

Tai Chi and Shaolin, taught by Ellen
Serber, 5:30 to 7 p.m.

Drawing from a Different Perspective,
taught by Mary Eubank, 7:30 to 10 p.m.

TUESDAYS

Aerobics and Body Conditioning, taught
by Vicki Verzuh, 8:30 to 9:30 a.m.

Stretch and Strengthen, taught by Nancy
Hemmingway, 9:30 to 10:30 a.m.

Hay barn
rebuilding

Point Reyes rancher George Nunes has
applied to the county for permission to build
a new hay barn. Nunes lost a barn last
December when hurricane-force winds
knocked it down. A deputy zoning admini-
strator will hold a hearing on Nunes’ applica-
tion Sept. 14.
