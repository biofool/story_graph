MILESTONES

Stonewall rioters defied
anti-gay raids: New York City
police were conducting a routine
raid the morning of June 28,
1969. The target was the
Stonewall Inn, a Greenwich Vil-
lage bar at 53 Christopher St. The
reason it was being raided: it was
a bar frequented by gay men.
Such vice raids were typical, but
the results of this one were differ-

ent. Instead of accepting the raid
as a matter of course, about 200
abl rioted, taunting police,

urling debris and setting small

fires. The disturbance lasted less
than an hour, but similar distur-
bances broke out on succeeding
nights. The Stonewall riots
became a turning point for the
gay-rights movement, and their
anniversary is marked by gay
activists in the United States and
other countries. Today, the con-
troversies over gay rights are far
from settled, involving such issues
as state sodomy laws, same-sex
marriages, medical benefits for
same-sex partners and anti-gay
violence and discrimination.
