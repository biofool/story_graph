Scott Family Y begins
aikido classes today

The Quad-City Aikido Club at the
Davenport and Scott County Family Y
will begin its fall session today.

Classes will be from 6:30-8 p.m.
Tuesdays and Thursdays and are
offered to men and women.

The cost is $17 for each seven-week
session. A Y membership is required,
but no one is turned: down for financial
need.

The Quad City Aikido Club is affili-
ated with the Midwest Aikido
Fedetation, the United Sfates Aikido
