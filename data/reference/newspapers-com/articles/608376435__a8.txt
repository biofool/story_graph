HE’S PLEASED — Yoshihio Asano is pleased with the
Shimano bicycle loaned to him this term by the ‘Berke.
ley-Sakai Sister City Assn. The bicycle was a gift to
the Association from its counterpart, the Sakai-Berke-
ley Sister City Committee, and was presented to the
association during the: visit of the Goodwill Mission
from Sakai last July. Asano is the third student in the
Graduate Exchange Program between the depart-
ments of landscape architecture of the University of
Osaka Prefecture in Sakai and the University of Cali.
fornia here. He will be a guest researcher, specializing
in urban design, for one year. The exchange is ad-
ministered by the Committee for International Educa.
tion. Patricia Gutter is the Berkeley Exchange Student
currently in Sakai. The Berkeley-Sakai Assn. partici-
pates in the program by providing host families for

the student.
