Board to vote on sick leave
law transition period

The Board of Supervisors
will vote on Tuesday whether to
establish a transition period in a
new paid sick leave law that went
into effect Monday.

Supervisor Sean Elsbernd drafted
legislation that would allow employ-
ers to not pay out any paid sick leave
until June 5, while still requiring
employers to track accrued sick
leave and allow workers to take paid
sick days. The delay is designed to
give The City and employers more
time to figure out the details of the
first-in-the-nation law.

The Board of Supervisors Rules
Committee voted on Thursday to
send the legislation to the full board
for a vote on Tuesday.

MTA

Central subway plan OKd
to compete for federal funds

The second proposed phase of the
Municipal Transportation Agency's
T-Third Street light rail program,
the Central Subway, is in line to
compete for federal New Starts
funds based on a rating given by the
Federal Transit Administration.

Approved for preliminary engi-
neering by the FTA, the project
would extend light-rail service
under the South of Market, Union
Square and Chinatown areas. The
subway has already received $45
million in federal funding and should
open by 2016.
