Q: Why do dogs in Texas need
to be vaccinated yearly? In Cali-
fornia, my dog got a vaccination
that was good for three years.
Isn’t the vaccination available in
Texas?

A: In Texas, state law requires
that dogs and cats be vaccinated
against rabies at four months of
age and have a booster vaccina-
tion every 12 months after the ini-
tial shot. There are rabies
vaccines licensed for one year
and some for as long as three
years.

It is impossible to monitor
which one is administered to a
dog or cat, so theoretically, in an
area where rabies vaccination is
required every three years, a pet
could be unprotected for a long pe-
riod of time.

Another reason that rabies vac-
cination is required once a year is
that officials in this area consider
the endemic strain of rabies virus,
the type of rabies virus present in
Texas, to be very virulent. A year-
ly vaccination helps give a routine
boost to the immune system. Over
a period of three years, it is diffi-
cult to determine how much of the
vaccinated population retains its
immunity.
