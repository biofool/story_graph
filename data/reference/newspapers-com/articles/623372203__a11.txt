SACRAMENTO BIKE HIKERS — Satur-
day, 10 a.m., meet at the corner of 35th
Street and McKinley Boulevard for a 27-
mile ride to Old Town Fair Oaks and a
stop at Sunflower Diner, Rusty or Patrice
452-3870; Sunday, 9 a.m., 35- and 54-

the Plymouth parking lot in downtown

Steve, 925-0307

9 a.m. All skill levels welcome, 442-8842.
