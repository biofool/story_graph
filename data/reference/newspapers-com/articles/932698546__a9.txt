For a reporter who’s used to covering

— from the pros down to junior high
athletes — Nobuo Iseri is refreshingly
different. Iseri teaches aikido, a Japa-

While aikido (pronounced I-key-doe) is
a-type of martial art, it is more closely
related to meditation. “The person who’s
thrown and the person who is doing the
throwing are one. There’s no intent of
harming an individual,” explained Iseri,
a nurse with the county.

According to Iseri, the goal of aikido is
to bring the spiritual and physical body
together.

Pretty heavy stuff for many sports fans
who are still trying to figure out what the
infield fly rule means or what a slotback
does.
