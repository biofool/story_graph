may be in person or by your
attorney.

IF YOU ARE A CREDITOR
or a contingent creditor of the
deceased, you must file your
claim with the court within
four months from the date of
the first issuance of letters
provided in section 9100 of
the California Probate Code.
The time for filing claims will
not expire before four months
from the date of the hearing
notice above.

person interested in the

court a formal request for
special notice of the filing of
an inventory and appraisal of
estate assets or of any peti-
tion or account as provided in
section 1250 of the California
Probate Code. A request for
special notice is available
from the court clerk.
