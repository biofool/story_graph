EXHIBITION: “5,000 Years of Korean Art";
@ major exhibition of 345 pieces, including
national treasures and art objects of gold
discovered in recent archaeological exca-
vations, opens Tuesday, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.,
Asian Art Museum of S.F., Golden Gate
Park. This is the largest exhibition of
Korean art to tour the United States.
Included are gold crowns, gold girdles,
jade pendants, Koryo celadon porcelains,
iron and stone sculptures, bronzes, paint-
ings, ceramics, tiles and other art objects.
(to Sept. 30).

CINCO DE MAYO: La Raza hosts week-
long celebration through Friday, May 4th,

speakers, theater. For details on the
programs, call 469-2016.
