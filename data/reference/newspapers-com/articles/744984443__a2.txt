Coast Karate Studio | offers new ‘Ki-.
Works’ Sunday sessions for adults.

Every Sunday beginning at noon,
Sensei Dave Bendigkeit of Coast
Karate Studio I will be offering a

weekly ‘‘Ki-Works” energy work-”

shop for adults. The sessions are ex-
cellent tools to’ increase your energy
level and awareness, These are not
martial arts classes, and include gentle
stretching, breathing meditation, cen-
tering training, Chi Gung exercises,
movement training, partner practice,
Ki extension, Shintaikido massage
and more.

Ki-Works is the name of the en-
ergy and healing aspects of
Shintaikido Jujitsu. It is a process of
healing the mind, body and spirit.
Ki-Works bases all of its practices on
energy flow in the human body. All
of these aspects are taught as parts of
a daily routine that you can do to
keep yourself in tune with your body’s
energy needs.

In 1967, Dave Bendigkeit began
his training in the martial art of Aikido
with Robert Nadeau Sensei. Nadeau
Sensei helped to encourage his inter-
est in the energy and healing aspects
of the martial arts.

Through years of study and prac-
tice in ki healing, shiatsu and tradi-
tional massage and movement train-
ing, Sensei Bendigkeit had devel-
oped Ki-Works, a system of energy
training that provides relief from
many of the stresses of modern day
life. He is a certified practitioner of
Chi-Gung training and the founder
of Shintaikido Jujitsu. He has con-
ducted numerous workshops
throughout the Bay Area. Sensei
Dave invites any interested adults to
join the class at noon Sundays or call
355-7661 with questions.

Coast Karate Studio I is located
at 150 Eureka Square.

Sensei Dave Bendigkeit
