WALKS/RUNS

FREE RUNNING CLINICS — The Sacra-
mento Long Distance Running Association
Offers free running clinics every Tuesday
and Thursday until the California Interna-
tional Marathon on Dec. 6. The Tuesday
clinic begins at 5:30 p.m. at Steve's Pizza,
813 Howe Avenue, and the Thursday clinic
begins at 6 p.m. at American River Hospital.
For more information, call 447-2786.

SUTTER BUTTES WALK — A variety of
walk/hike programs are available through
February. Call Ira Heinrich in Chico (916)
343-6614.

TRICK-OR-TROT — Saturday, two-mile
and 10k routes, Incline Village, 960 Lake-
shore Drive, check-in 9 a.m., (702) 832-
1225

VOLKSMARCH — Nov. 7, 7:30 a.m. to
12:30 p.m., Meadow Vista Park, free, 965-
4343.

STATE PARK FALL HIKE — Nov. 7, 10
a.m., moderate to difficult 5-hour hike from
Carson Pass Summit on Highway 88 to Red
Lake Peak, bring camera, good hiking
boots, lunch and water, (916) 694-2248.

VOLKSMARCH — Nov. 14 and 15, spon-
sored by Sutter Strutters, 6.2 miles starting
at El Dorado Orchards, North Canyon Road
in Camino from 8 a.m. to 1 p.m. Free. 487-
1725 or 451-5224.

ECHOES OF THE LAND — Day hike ($20)
on Nov. 14 and Nature and Scenic Photog-
raphy ($40) on Nov. 15, $5 deposit by Nov.
7, sponsored by West Butte Sanctuary
Company in Woodland. Call Allan, 756-
6283 after 4 p.m.

BICYCLING

SACRAMENTO BIKE HIKERS — Orienta-
tion ride Saturday at 9 a.m., meet at the
Campus Commons end of the Guy West
Bridge and ride to Old Sacramento, 723-
3126. Sunday at 9:30 a.m., a 50-mile ride
and brunch, meet at Newcastle Park & Ride
off 1-80, 624-9112. Club Meeting, Nov. 4, 7
p.m. at Mercy San Juan Conference Center
No. 4. Visitors welcome.

SACRAMENTO WHEELMEN — Cameron
Park Evening Ride tonight, Cameron Park
exit off Hwy 50, 5:30 p.m., meet across
from Burger King, 726-8486. “Spooky
Ride,” Saturday, 10 a.m., 20 miles, meet at
3435 J Street for this easy but haunting
ride, 444-8051. Rocklin Ride on Sunday, 10
a.m., meet at Rocklin Bicycle Company,
30-40 miles in the foothills, bring water,
488-8218.

SACRAMENTO GOLDEN WHEELMEN —
Ongoing activities include bicycle racing,
race training and fast group rides, 447-
8310.

CBW CYCLERS — Meets Saturday morn-
ings at 9 a.m., City Bicycle Works, 2419 K
Street, for fast recreational rides. Ask for
Jess, 447-2453.

ROUGH RIDERS — Non-competitive moun-
tain-bike club. All skill levels welcome, 447-
2453 weekends.

LAKE MEADOWS MOUNTAIN RIDE —
Nov. 14, three hours of mostly flat terrain
for views of Mt. Tallac, Freel Peak and An-
gora Peak. Offered through the California
State Park system. Meet at the Lake Tahoe
Country Club parking lot in Meyers near
Hwy 50. Bring a camera and mountain bike.
(916) 525-7277.
