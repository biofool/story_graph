Skiers unite! —— For too long, a Sierra
Club skier-activist warns, those who tour the win-
ter wilderness have been taking only pictures and
leaving only tracks.

“Places where we used to ski are becoming
housing developments, locked up by resorts or un-
der study for inclusion into alpine ski areas,” Mia
James of San Mateo says. And that — public ac-
cess to public lands — is considered the number
one issue concerning backcountry skiers in Cali-
fornia.

As a result, James and a several other Bay Area
skiers are taking steps to promote the sport. First,
they've just published a newsletter, Nordic Voice.

Next, they plan to form a “lobbying, working ated
playing organization” — eventually, non- profit
and statewide — that advances the interests of
wilderness skiers.

Meantime, the grass-roots effort, with Jamesas
chairperson, is affiliated with the ski touring sec»
tion of the Sierra Club's Loma Prieta Chapter. ;

To receive a free copy of Nordic Voice and,to
be placed on the group's mailing list, write: Nor-
dic Voice, 3383 Burgundy Drive, San Jose, 95132.

Shoes of winter — A quiet revolution has
been going on in the winter wilderness, reports
Jim Chase, in Backpacker magazine.

“Snowshoes, whose death was foretold a decade
ago, have not only continued to hold sway in the
rugged backcountry, they have been improved
and found new converts.

“Actually, snowshoes have skis to thanks for
some of their current popularity. What people
wanted was not the long, stable, distance-running
shoes. Skis did that just fine. So the new, ‘Western’
showshoes came along. They lost their stems: in
the rear and ended up generally smaller.” ;

New materials, such as aluminum tubing and
neoprene cording and sheeting, also helped.to
revolutionize this age-old piece of outdoor equip-
ment. Chase calls them “light, compact and effi-
cient,” adding that they're also “easily strapped to
the back of a pack, and even more easily UNSLTADy
ped and placed on the foot.”

The price, of course, matches the space-age
look — up to $150 to $300 for a technically ad-
vanced shoe.
