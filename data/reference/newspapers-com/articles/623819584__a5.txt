2. Eagle Lake: The moon is on
the wane and the fishing’s on the
rise, Fish are running from. 5 to 7
pounds with a few smaller fish
showing. Trollers are going six to
10 feet down with a smail flasher
and a night crawler between Rocky
Point and Pelican Point. Use an 8-
to 10-inch leader with 6- to 8-pound
test, threading the crawler onto a
No. 4 long-shank hook. Bank fish-
ermen are using a bobber with a
split shot and a threaded-on crawl-
er. Best fishing has been from 10
am. to2 p.m. 825-2191.
