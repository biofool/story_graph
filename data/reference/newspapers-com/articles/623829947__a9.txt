SACRAMENTO BIKE HIKERS — Saturday,

EL DORADO CYCLING CLUB — 3rd annual
Polar Bear Century on Jan. 16, 6:30 a.m. at
Markham Junior High in Placerville; rain,
snow or shine, $15 registration, 50K, 100K
and 100-mile routes through the Sierra Ne-
vada foothills. Lunch, dinner and sag sup-
port provided, (916) 626-8370.
