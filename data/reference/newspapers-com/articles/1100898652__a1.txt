As a Friend of the Dance Palace you will receive: A quarterly brochure
featuring a calendar of events * A members’ newsletter with further information
about events, classes, and policies of the Dance Palace %* Voting rights to pick
your Board of Directors annually and the right to give your views and suggestions

on programs and events at any time * A membership card good for free introductory
classes for almost any Dance Palace course offered during the year * Free tickets
to a movie of your choice in the Dance Palace film series (2 for individuals, 4 for
families) %* A copy of the Dance Palace annual report, with more complete
information on our current program and budget, and our future financial needs + Priority
on ticket purchase for special events such as Dance Palace Day at the Oakland
Coliseum AND an exciting New Year's Party * Your name, as an individual or
business. posted prominently on our Friends list during all Dance Palace events and
classes % Discounts on rental sales for classes, events, and equipment %* The
Satisfaction of supporting the liveliest community art center in Marin County!
