Audubon Society Meets Today

The Venice Area Audubon Society will meet at 7:30 p.m,
today at St. Mark’s Episcopal Church, on Riviera Street.

The program will include nature slides by Jason J.
Schneider of Englewood Isles.

The meeting is open to the public. A report will be
given on the Florida Audubon Society by Mrs. Mary Stolte,
president.

A field trip will be held Wednesday at 8:30 a.m. from
the city parking lot, at Nokomis and West Venice Avenue

to visit shore areas in Englewood. Participants should bring
a lunch for the trip.
