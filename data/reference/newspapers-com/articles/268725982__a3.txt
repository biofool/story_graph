BROWNSVILLE

Christian school
plans fish fry

Escambia Christian
School’s annual fish fry is
planned for Oct. 13 from
4:30 to 7 p.m. at the school
gym, 3311 W. Moreno St.

A silent auction will take
place inside the gym dur-
ing the event.

The Fish Fry menu will
include fried mullet, baked
beans, cole slaw, hush pup-
pies, tea, coffee and
dessert. Cost: students $3
in advance, or $5 at the
door, adults $5 in advance
or $7 at the door.
