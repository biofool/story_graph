Eradicating child abuse and sexual assault is an on-

going process. The Child Sexual Abuse Project (a program of
the Tompkins County Task Force for Battered Women) joins
other communities in scheduling special events and projects
in recognition this April’s Child Abuse and Sexual Assault Pre-
vention Month. The Ithaca Journal will pubish selected posters
created by sixth-graders of DeWitt and Boynton middle
schools who look to educate other youth and

adults about sexual abuse and rape. For
more information about abuse and rape, April
events, or the Child Sexual Abuse Project,
please call Jennifer Rosenthal, education
director of CSAP at 277-3203. For questions
about “I like to draw...” please contact
Ronson Slagle at 274-9284 or e-mail:
ithaca@timewarp.gannett.com
