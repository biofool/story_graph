San Carlos recreation
Officials have stated that its
Holiday Fun program _ this
year has been well received
by participants — boys and
girls from 6 to 9 years of age.

Recreation leaders Patricia
Barrett and Carol Trumbull
conduct such activities as
Christmas crafts, cookie mak-
ing, games, stores, caroling, a
trip to the Circle Star Theatre,
and a Christmas party.

Programs for 1968 will begin
Jan, 2.

A baton instruction program
will be offered for girls in
grades three to five. Instruc-
tor will be Sandy Horner.
Classes will open Tuesday,
Jan. 2, then will meet on Mon-
days until Jan. 29 from 4 to 5
p.m. in the Kiwanis Commu-
nity Recreation Center, Bur-
ton Park.

Girls may register now for
the program at the recreation
department administration of-
fices at Burton Park. There is
no fee for the program. The
class will be limited to the
first 15 registrants.

The park and recreation de- .

partment in cooperation with
the San Carlos Civil Defense
Radio Club, will begin its sec-
ond radio code class for
youngsters 10 years and older.
It is designed to train the
youngsters sufficiently to ac-
quire their amateur novice
Class licenses.

The class will open at 8 p.m.
Monday, Jan. 15, at San Car-
los Fire Station No. 2, 1101 In-
dustrial Way. Bill Burris,
WA6CXJ, radio officer for San
Carlos Civil Defense, may be
contacted for further informa-
tion at 591-4592.

The department will offer a
puppetry program from Jan.
23, through March 19.

Youngsters 7 to 10 years old
will meet every Tuesday from
3:30 to 5 p.m. at Burton Park
to design and construct their
own puppets. They also will

help write the script for a pup- —

pet show that will highlight
the program. Theme of the
show will be decided by the
participants.

Registration now is being
accepted at Burton Park for a
10-week program in yoga in-
Struction.

Sessions will meet Mondays
from 10 to 11 a.m. at Burton
Park, beginning Jan. 8; Wed-
nesdays from 7 to 8 p.m. at
Laureola Park, beginning Jan.
10, and Fridays from 1 to 2
p.m. at Burton Park, begin-
ning Jan. 12.

Mrs. Ann Ericksen will di-
rect the program. Participants
must supply their own mats.

Robert Nadeau instructor in
the Oriental art of aikido, a
method of self-defense for men
and women — will direct a
program for the recreation de-
partment, beginning Jan. 25.

Nadeau said that Aikido is
not a violent activity, as com-
pared with karate, jui-jitsu or
judo, but still provides persons
with a useful means of self-
defense. Classes will meet
Thursdays from 7 to 9 p.m. in
the wrestling room at San Car-
los High. Adults may register
at the recreation department
office in Burton Park. for the
eight-week course.
