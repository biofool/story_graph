Richard Moon

You are here: Home » Network » Nautilus Associates » Richard Moon

Richard Moon founded ExtraOrdinaryListening.com, focused on improving human relations andeffectiveness, and is a senior associate with The Nautilus Institute.

A yoga instructor for over40 years, Richard Moon is also renowned as an innovative Aikido Instructor holding the rank of 5th Degree Black Belt with Aikido World Headquarters and has taught around the world. He is an executive coach to corporations teaching senior level executives as well as facilitating trainings in team development.

He has engaged in international peace building in Cypress and Bosnia and has authored several books, including The Power of Extraordinary Listening and Aikido in Three Easy Lessons, which is also available as a DVD. He also is a musician and produces of a variety of musical recordings.
