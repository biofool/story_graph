Quantum Aikido

The Power of Harmony

Quantum Aikido: The Power of Harmony is a distillation of principles and practices gathered over sixty years of study in Aikido, yoga, music, and various awareness arts. Rather than focusing on martial techniques for combat, the book explores how to develop our own spirit to fulfill our lives in the way that is most natural to who we are. It bridges the principles of Aikido with the insights of quantum physics and the improvisational nature of jazz, offering a unified lens through which we can view health, resilience, creativity, and our relationships with others. The core of the work is the understanding that we are here to manifest our greatest power by winning over the discord within our own minds, ultimately creating a beautiful world through a spirit of harmony.

What is Quantum Aikido?

Quantum Aikido: The Power of Harmony is a commercially published book by aikido teacher Richard Moon, released by Inner Traditions / Park Street Press. It reveals how using the space between stimulus and response, we can access infinite creative possibility, where opposites complete each other. The book bridges the principles of Aikido with the insights of quantum physics and the improvisational nature of jazz, offering a unified lens through which we can view health, resilience, creativity, and our relationships with others.
