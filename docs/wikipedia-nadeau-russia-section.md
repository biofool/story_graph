### Seminars in the Soviet Union

```wikitext
=== Seminars in the Soviet Union ===

In October 1990, Nadeau visited the Soviet Union at the invitation of aikidoka
from Moscow. On October 27, 1990 — the day of the founding conference of the
USSR Aikido Federation — he conducted a training session at the [[Lenkai]]
Aikido Club in [[Leningrad]] (now [[Saint Petersburg]]). At the time of the
visit, Nadeau held the rank of 6th [[dan (rank)|dan]].<ref name="aikiclub-spb">{{cite web
  | url = http://www.aikiclub.ru/spb.html
  | title = Развитие Айкидо в Санкт-Петербурге (Development of Aikido in St. Petersburg)
  | author = V. Matveev
  | publisher = Moscow Aiki Club
  | date = 2003-01-13
  | language = ru
  | access-date = 2026-09-08
  | quote = 27 октября 1990 года, в день проведения Учредительной конференции Федерации Айкидо СССР, в клубе "Ленкай" провел тренировку Роберт НАДО (6-й Дан, США), посетивший Советский Союз по приглашению айкидок из Москвы.
}}</ref>

Nadeau's visit was part of a broader exchange between American and Soviet
aikidoka in the late 1980s and early 1990s. Other American instructors who
taught in the USSR during this period included [[Jack Wada]] and Sherry Dyer

(May 1990).<ref name="aikiclub-spb" />

Instructors from the Federal Alliance of Bujutsu Russia (Kazan) independently
recalled training with Nadeau alongside other visiting masters during the
1987–1994 period in Moscow and Leningrad.<ref name="bujutsu-aikido">{{cite web
```

## Sources

### Primary independent source

**Moscow Aiki Club — History of Aikido in St. Petersburg**
- URL: http://www.aikiclub.ru/spb.html
- Author: V. Matveev
- Date: January 13, 2003
- Language: Russian
- Independence: Moscow Aiki Club is an independent organization with no affiliation to Nadeau
- Key quote (Russian): "27 октября 1990 года, в день проведения Учредительной конференции Федерации Айкидо СССР, в клубе "Ленкай" провел тренировку Роберт НАДО (6-й Дан, США), посетивший Советский Союз по приглашению айкидок из Москвы."
- Key quote (English): "On October 27, 1990, the day of the founding conference of the USSR Aikido Federation, Robert NADO (6th dan, USA) conducted a training at the Lenkai club, having visited the Soviet Union at the invitation of aikidoka from Moscow."
- What it confirms: date (Oct 27, 1990), location (Lenkai club, Leningrad), name (Robert Nado), rank (6th dan), nationality (USA), context (founding conference of USSR Aikido Federation)
- What it does NOT confirm: who traveled with Nadeau (states he visited "at the invitation of aikidoka from Moscow" but does not name travel companions)

### Secondary independent source

**Federal Alliance of Bujutsu Russia — Aikido page**
- URL: https://www.bujutsu.ru/aikido/
- Publisher: Federal Alliance of Bujutsu Russia (Kazan)
- Language: Russian
- Independence: Independent of Nadeau
- Key quote (Russian): "Александру Леонидовичу и Алексею Георгиевичу, именно в тот период времени, посчастливилось тренироваться у таких известных мастеров Айкидо, как Джек Вада (7 Дан Aikido Aikikai США), Роберт Надо (6 Дан Aikido Aikikai, Канада), Кристиан Тисьер (7 Дан Aikido Aikikai, Франция) и многих других."
- Key quote (English): "Alexander Leonidovich and Alexey Georgievich, during that period, had the good fortune to train with such famous Aikido masters as Jack Wada (7 dan Aikido Aikikai USA), Robert Nado (6 dan Aikido Aikikai, Canada), Christian Tissier (7 dan Aikido Aikikai, France) and many others."
- What it confirms: Nadeau taught seminars in Moscow/Leningrad during 1987–1994
- Discrepancy: incorrectly lists nationality as Canada (should be USA)
- What it does NOT confirm: specific dates, who traveled with Nadeau

### Context: other American visitors to the USSR (same era)

From the same aikiclub.ru page, the following American aikido instructors visited the USSR in the years immediately before and after Nadeau's visit. These are listed as separate visits, not as traveling companions of Nadeau:

| Date | Visitor | Rank | Context |
|------|---------|------|---------|
| Autumn 1989 | Jamie Zimron | 3 dan | Delegation of American aikido masters |
| Winter 1989–1990 | Sherry Dyer, Jack Wada | 3 dan, 5 dan | Seminar in Moscow |
| Late May 1990 | Koichi Berish | — | Delegation from America |
| **October 27, 1990** | **Robert Nado (Nadeau)** | **6 dan** | **Lenkai club, Leningrad** |
| Late autumn 1990 | Danish aikido masters | — | — |
| June 1991 | Kitaura sensei | — | First Aikikai shihan to visit the USSR |

**Note:** The aikiclub.ru page does not state that Nadeau traveled with any of these other visitors. Each appears to be a separate visit. The page states Nadeau visited "at the invitation of aikidoka from Moscow" but does not name who traveled with him.

## Travel companions

**No source found that documents who traveled with Nadeau to the USSR.** The aikiclub.ru page states he visited "at the invitation of aikidoka from Moscow" (по приглашению айкидок из Москвы) but does not name any travel companions. The bujutsu.ru page mentions Nadeau alongside Jack Wada and Christian Tissier as masters whose seminars were attended, but this does not indicate they traveled together — these appear to be separate visits by different instructors during the same broader period (1987–1994).
