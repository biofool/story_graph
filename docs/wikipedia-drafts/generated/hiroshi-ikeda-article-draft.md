== Career ==

Hiroshi Ikeda founded Boulder Aikikai, Aikido Bridge.<ref name="ref8"/>

Premillieu trained under Ikeda in Boulder; AfT created at his encouragement<ref name="ref9"/>

Bill McIntyre invited Saotome to Florida in 1976; Ikeda followed as his assistant (having planned to go to Australia)<ref name="ref8"/>

The Sarasota dojo where Ikeda taught 1976-79 was the '12th Street Dojo'<ref name="ref2"/>

First virtual Aikido Bridge seminar (May 2020) hosted by Shimbokukai, Ikeda closing<ref name="ref10"/>

Ikeda joined Aikido Shimbokukai as vice president in 2016 under Lisa Tomoleoni<ref name="ref8"/>

In early 2015 Saotome recognized Ikeda as an 'independent instructor', ending Ikeda's ASU association<ref name="ref8"/>

Hiroshi Ikeda was born in January 1950 on Hachijojima (Hachijo Island), Tokyo Metropolis, Japan<ref name="ref2"/>

Ikeda trained judo for three years in high school before taking up aikido<ref name="ref8"/>

Ikeda at Aikido of Madison in the 1980s (visit, not affiliation)<ref name="ref11"/>

Boulder Aikikai was established August 1980<ref name="ref2"/>

Ikeda served as Vice President of Saotome's Aikido Schools of Ueshiba<ref name="ref8"/>

Aikido Bridge seminar series corroborated in AJ-published material: 10th Anniversary San Diego (~2015), Turkey Friendship Seminar 2018, Boulder 'Aikido for Tomorrow' spinoff 2020<ref name="ref1"/>

Sources conflict on Ikeda's aikido start year (1968 vs 1969) and Reimei Juku entry (1970 vs 1971)<ref name="ref2"/>

Circa 1995 Ikeda's annual circuit: 2-3 seminar weekends/month + San Rafael (Nadeau/Doran), D.C. (Saotome), and own Rocky Mountain summer camps<ref name="ref3"/>
