## 3. Proposal for Talk:Robert Nadeau (aikidoka)

### COI disclosure

I am connected to the subject: I personally know Robert Nadeau, and the
research project behind this proposal contains my own recollections about
Nadeau-era dojo history. None of that personal knowledge is used as a
source for the wording below — it is disclosed here only for transparency.

### Cited sources only

1. **V. Matveev, "Развитие Айкидо в Санкт-Петербурге" (Development of Aikido
   in St. Petersburg), Moscow Aiki Club, 2003-01-13** —
   http://www.aikiclub.ru/spb.html (Russian). Contemporaneous club-history
   record naming "Роберт НАДО (6-й Дан, США)" conducting a training at the
   Lenkai club on the day of the USSR Aikido Federation's founding
   conference. Organizational source — cited with attribution.
2. **Sergey Kiselev, "Айкидо в СССР", Lenkai Aikido Club history** —
   https://lenkai.spb.ru/ajkido-v-sssr/ (Russian). First-person account by
   the Lenkai founder and first USSR Aikido Federation president. Archival /
   primary.
3. **Federal Alliance of Bujutsu Russia (Kazan), "Айкидо"** —
   https://bujutsu.ru/aikido/ and https://rebenok-na-aikido.ru/ (Russian).
   Recalls training with Nadeau alongside Jack Wada and Christian Tissier
   in the 1987–1994 period. (Lists his nationality as Canada — an error in
   the source; he is American.)
4. **Jamie Zimron interview, BigRock Aikikai (Jan 2022)** —
   https://www.bigrock-aikikai.com/ferocious-and-female/interview-jamie-zimron-sensei-(6th-dan).html
   — corroborates the American–Soviet aikido exchange period (1987–1991).
5. **Kannagara Aikido Moscow, Koichi Barrish biography** —
   http://www.kannagara-aikido.ru/mentors/KoichiBarrish/ — Barrish's Moscow
   seminars from 1987 onward, independent timeline context.

### Explicit non-use statement

## 4. Policy checklist

- **WP:V** — every proposed sentence carries a specific citation with a
  verifiable URL and quoted original-language text.
- **WP:NOR** — no synthesis: the "broader exchange" sentence is separately
  cited to the Bujutsu federation's own recollection; no claim links two
  sources beyond what either states.
- **WP:NPOV** — attributed register ("According to the Moscow Aiki Club's
  history…", "recall") matches the primary/organizational character of the
  sources; the source's "Canada" nationality error is flagged here, not
  silently corrected into the article.
- **WP:RS** — weaknesses stated openly: organizational sources lack
  editorial independence, so wording is attributed rather than asserted in
  Wikipedia's voice; the affiliated source (cityaikido.com) is excluded
  from the proposal in favor of independent ones.
