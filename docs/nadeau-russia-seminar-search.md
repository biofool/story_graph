**Date:** 2026-08-26

## 1. Summary

**Key finding:** A significant independent source was found. The Moscow Aiki Club website (aikiclub.ru) contains a detailed history of aikido in St. Petersburg/Leningrad that explicitly names "Роберт НАДО (6-й Дан, США)" conducting a training session at the Lenkai club in Leningrad on **October 27, 1990**, the day of the founding conference of the USSR Aikido Federation. This is an independent, third-party Russian martial arts organization's historical account — not from Nadeau's own sites or affiliates.

Additionally, the Federal Alliance of Bujutsu Russia (bujutsu.ru, based in Kazan) independently mentions "Роберт Надо" as one of the masters whose seminars were attended by their instructors during 1987–1994 in Moscow, Leningrad, and other Soviet cities.

**What was found:**
- 1 strong independent source (aikiclub.ru) with specific date, location, and context
- 1 moderate independent source (bujutsu.ru / rebenok-na-aikido.ru) confirming seminar attendance in Moscow/Leningrad during the relevant period
- Multiple Nadeau-affiliated sources (cityaikido.com, aikidoofpetaluma.com, aikido.org.nz, ai-ki-do.org) confirming he taught in Russia, but these are not independent
- Aikido Journal (aikidojournal.com) mentions Nadeau's influence in Russia, but the statement appears to originate from his own bio
- No coverage found for Riga specifically
- No English-language news or magazine reports from the early 1990s

**What was NOT found:**
- No independent confirmation of seminars in Riga
- No English-language news articles or magazine reports from the early 1990s
- No photographs or video from the Russia seminars
- No mention of Nadeau in the official Russian aikido federation timelines (aikido.ru, en.aikido.ru, jikishinkai.ru) — though these timelines focus on Hombu Dojo shihan visits, not visiting Western instructors
- The Wikipedia article on Nadeau lists teaching in "United States, Switzerland, Israel and New Zealand" — notably omitting Russia

## 6. Detailed Analysis of Key Source

### aikiclub.ru/spb.html — Moscow Aiki Club

**URL:** http://www.aikiclub.ru/spb.html
**Page title:** "Развитие Айкидо в Санкт-Петербурге" (Development of Aikido in St. Petersburg)
**Author:** V. Matveev, Moscow Aiki Club
**Date:** 13.01.2003

**Key passage (from search snippet):**

> 27 октября 1990 года, в день проведения Учредительной конференции Федерации Айкидо СССР, в клубе "Ленкай" провел тренировку Роберт НАДО ( 6-й Дан, США ), посетивший Советский Союз по приглашению айкидок из Москвы.

**Translation:**

> On October 27, 1990, the day of the founding conference of the USSR Aikido Federation, Robert NADO (6th dan, USA) conducted a training at the Lenkai club, having visited the Soviet Union at the invitation of aikidoka from Moscow.

**Context from the same page:**

The page provides a detailed chronological history of aikido in Leningrad/St. Petersburg, listing numerous visiting instructors:
- Autumn 1989: Jamie Zimron (3 dan) delegation of American aikido masters
- Winter 1989-90: Sherry Dyer (3 dan) and Jack Wada (5 dan) seminar in Moscow
- Late May 1990: Koichi Berish delegation from America
- June 1990: Jacques Bonemaison (6 dan, France)
- **October 27, 1990: Robert Nado (6 dan, USA)**
- Late autumn 1990: Danish aikido masters
- 1990: Lyubomir Vracharevich (5 dan)
- December 1990: Vladimir Tagirov visits Kitaura sensei in Madrid
- June 1991: Kitaura sensei seminar in Leningrad (first Aikikai shihan in USSR)

**Why this is significant for Wikipedia notability:**

1. **Independent source:** Moscow Aiki Club is an established Russian martial arts organization with no ties to Nadeau
2. **Specific date:** October 27, 1990 — the founding conference of the USSR Aikido Federation
3. **Specific location:** Lenkai club, Leningrad (St. Petersburg)
4. **Correct identification:** Lists him as 6th dan, USA — matching Nadeau's rank at that time (he was promoted to 7th dan later)
5. **Historical significance:** The founding conference of the USSR Aikido Federation was a major event in Russian martial arts history
6. **Corroborating context:** The page lists many other visiting instructors from the same era, providing a credible historical framework

**Discrepancy with bujutsu.ru:**

The bujutsu.ru page lists "Роберт Надо (6 Дан Aikido Aikikai, **Канада**)" — incorrectly stating Canada as his nationality. The aikiclub.ru page correctly states "США" (USA). This suggests the bujutsu.ru authors may have confused Nadeau with another instructor or made an error, but the name and rank match.

**Rank discrepancy:**

- aikiclub.ru: 6th dan (1990) — consistent with Nadeau's timeline
- bujutsu.ru: 6th dan — same
- rebenok-na-aikido.ru: 7th dan — may reflect a later update or error
- wikiital.com: 7th dan — Wikipedia-derived
- Nadeau's current rank: 8th dan
