# Person outreach — Richard Moon's aikido teaching circle

Per-person email drafts asking individuals to **confirm or correct** a
suspected connection to Richard Moon Sensei's aikido teaching. Unlike the
organization drafts in `docs/outreach/moon/` (which cite documented seminar
records), these are **speculative** — the basis for each contact is stated
explicitly in the draft, and every email asks for a correction as readily
as a confirmation.

This wording is deliberate. The earlier Ikeda outreach asserted
"our records show X taught at your organization" and produced a false
claim to Kristina Varjan (issue #65). These drafts never assert an
unverified relationship — they say why we're asking and invite "no" as
a valid answer.

**Richard Moon and Rochelle Moon are direct contacts** (kkron knows them
personally) — no speculative email needed. These drafts target the circle
*around* him.
