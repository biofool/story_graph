Dear Dr. Diamond,

My name is Ken Kron. I'm researching and documenting the aikido teaching
career of Richard Moon Sensei (6th dan, Aikido of Marin / City Aikido of
San Francisco) for a biographical project — a sourced historical record of
his seminars, students, and guest teaching over the past five decades.

Our records indicate Moon worked with you through the Institute for
Multi-Track Diplomacy — beginning at the first IMTD Lake Trails camp in
1999, and later in Cyprus and Bosnia — where he taught aikido as a
conflict-resolution practice. I'd like to confirm that record and, if
you're willing, hear what you remember of his teaching in that context.

I'd be grateful for anything you can share:

- How Moon came to work with IMTD and what his role was
- Where and when he taught aikido (or aikido-derived exercises) in the
  peace-building work — dates, places, participants
- How participants received his teaching — anything quotable
- Program records, reports, photos, or correspondence you may have kept
- Others from that work I might contact

If I've got the wrong person, or the connection isn't as described, a short
correction is just as valuable as a confirmation — I'd rather record an
accurate "no" than publish a wrong guess.

Everything collected is being preserved as source material for an accurate
public record of the teaching career. I'm happy to share the compiled
history with you when complete, and to credit you as a source.

Warm regards,
Ken Kron
