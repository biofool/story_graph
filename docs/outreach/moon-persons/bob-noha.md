Dear Mr. Noha,

My name is Ken Kron. I'm researching and documenting the aikido teaching
career of Richard Moon Sensei (6th dan, Aikido of Marin / City Aikido of
San Francisco) for a biographical project — a sourced historical record of
his seminars, students, and guest teaching over the past five decades.

You co-authored *Aikido: The Art of Transformation* (2024) with Moon and
four others, and your own teaching history with Nadeau Sensei goes back
decades. What I'm hoping you can help with is the part that may not be in
the book — and I should be clear that the specifics of how closely you
trained *with* Moon, versus alongside him under Nadeau Sensei, are exactly
what I'm trying to pin down.

I'd be grateful for anything you can share:

- How you knew Moon — training together, seminars, the Nadeau circle
- Seminars or classes you remember him teaching (dates, places, hosts)
- His dan promotions and teaching milestones, if you recall them
- Students or training partners of his you remember — including anyone
  who crossed between your dojo and his
- Flyers, photos, or correspondence you may have kept
- Anyone else you think I should contact

If I've misread the connection, a short correction is just as valuable as
a confirmation — I'd rather record an accurate "no" than publish a wrong
guess.

Everything collected is being preserved as source material for an accurate
public record of the teaching career. I'm happy to share the compiled
history with you when complete, and to credit you as a source.

Warm regards,
Ken Kron
